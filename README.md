What Is Digital Marketing?
Digital marketing refers to promoting products and services through online platforms such as Google, social media, websites, email, and search engines. It helps businesses reach their target audience, generate leads, increase brand awareness, and improve sales.
Some important areas of digital marketing include:
Search Engine Optimization (SEO)
Social Media Marketing (SMM)
Google Ads and Pay-Per-Click Advertising
Content Marketing
Email Marketing
Website Development
Google Analytics
Online Reputation Management
Understanding these techniques helps learners develop practical skills that are useful in today's competitive business environment.
